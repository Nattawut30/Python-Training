**Data Sources**
-
* An ML system can work with data from many different sources.
* They have different characteristics, can be used for different purposes, and require different processing methods.
* Understanding the sources your data comes from can help you use your data more efficiently.
* one source is "user input data", data explicitly input by users.
* Users also have little patience.
* Another one is "System-generated data", this is the data generrated by different components of your systems.
* Logs can record the state and significant events of the system, such as memory usage, number of instances, services called, packages used.
* Logs are system generated, much less likely to be malformatted the way user input data is.
* Log everything you can!
* now it might be hard to look at and how to store it rapidly growing number of logs.
* Store it as long as they useful.
* "Internal database" generated by various services and enterprise application in a company.
* First-Party Data = the data that your company already collects about your users or customers.
* Second-Party Data = the data collected by another company on their own customers that they make available to you, you have to pay for it.
* Third-Party Data = Companies collect data on the public who aren't their direct customers.
